# Demo guide

## Start without gallery access

1. Launch Smart Memory.
2. Complete or skip onboarding.
3. On **Your gallery, your choice**, tap **Explore with demo data**.
4. The dashboard opens with a visible **Demo mode** banner.

No Android media permission is required for this path.

The “14 months since view” statement is a supplied illustrative demo assumption,
not a claim derived from the event date or real usage telemetry.

## Suggested demonstration flow

1. Review dashboard summary cards and the memory-needing-attention card.
2. Open **Story timeline** and show the editable automatic template label.
3. Open the event and explain all four score chips and factor contributions.
4. Use **Correct event** to rename, rate, favourite, choose a cover, split, or merge.
5. Add/remove a manual person tag; emphasize that contacts and automatic identity
   assignment are not used.
6. Add a written story or record a private voice note on a device.
7. Open the typed memory graph and select the event/person nodes.
8. Open the preservation inbox and follow an exact missing-context action.
9. Schedule a future capsule; show the neutral notification wording.
10. Mark the event sensitive and show that proactive recommendations disappear.
11. Open privacy/data management and explain that deletion affects derived app data
    only, never the gallery originals.

## Expected explanation

> This appears to be an important academic memory. It has not been opened for 14
> months and is missing personal context. Add a short voice note or story, or
> schedule it as a future time capsule.

Paraphrasing in the UI is acceptable as long as importance, low rediscovery,
missing context, and suggested actions remain explicit.

## Integrity statement

The values demonstrate UI and deterministic rule behavior. They are not trained
predictions, calibrated probabilities, or evidence of real-world effectiveness.
